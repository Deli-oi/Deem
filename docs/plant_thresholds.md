# Plant healthy ranges and out-of-range values

Generated by `data/plants/thresholds_report.py` from `demo/public/plants/seed_all.json`. Do not edit by hand.

A reading **outside** a plant's healthy range raises an alert (sound + on-screen message). The ranges come from MiFloraDB (Xiaomi Flower Care), i.e. the extremes the plant tolerates, not an ideal range, so every alert is **critical**: the plant is already being harmed. Small drifts never alert.

## Air quality (same limits for every plant)

The plant data has no per-species CO2 or dust ranges, so the SEN63C readings use blanket limits (`demo/src/lib/airQuality.ts`). They are set high on purpose, so a busy room does not set off the alert constantly.

| Reading | Alerts above | Alert text | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `co2_ppm` | 1500 ppm | CO2 critically high | Clearly stale air. Outdoors is ~420, a normal room 600-1000, 1000 is the usual poor-ventilation guideline. |
| `pm2_5` | 35 ug/m3 | Fine dust critically high | US EPA 24-hour limit (WHO guideline is stricter, 15). |
| `pm10_0` | 150 ug/m3 | Dust critically high | US EPA 24-hour limit. |

Low values never alert. `pm1_0` and `pm4_0` have no standard, so they never alert. A plant-specific range, if one is ever added, overrides these.
